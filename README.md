# Open Circle — Community Registration & Survey

A standalone HTML registration & survey form for **Open Circle**, a community by **OpenSkool** where curious minds explore AI, experiment with technology, share knowledge, and build practical skills.

🌐 **Live:** [vfxkaotaku.github.io/openskool](https://vfxkaotaku.github.io/openskool)

---

## Features

- 📋 4-step multi-page survey form
- 🤖 AI experience, tools, models & use cases selection
- 📊 Auto-submits to Google Sheets on completion
- 📱 Fully responsive — works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- ✅ Client-side validation on required fields
- 🎨 OpenSkool dark & neon green branding
- 💬 WhatsApp community invite on success

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Main survey form (rename from `open_circle_registration.html`) |
| `open_circle_logo.png` | Logo displayed on the form |
| `GoogleAppsScript.js` | Paste into Google Apps Script to connect your Sheet |
| `GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md` | Step-by-step Google Sheets connection guide |

## Deployment

This site is deployed via **GitHub Pages** from the `main` branch root.

## Google Sheets Integration

See [`GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md`](GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md) for full instructions on connecting form submissions to your Google Spreadsheet.
